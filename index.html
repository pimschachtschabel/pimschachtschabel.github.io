Keynote Speaker · Experience Designer · Author

What if curiosity isn't something you need more of, but something you need room for?

I'm Pim Schachtschabel. I design the conditions where curiosity comes alive, at the scale of a World Expo pavilion or a team of twelve.

Book Pim to Speak See the Work

Everyone is curious.
Not every room lets them be.

Curiosity is about discovery. That's the whole point of it. What I design isn't the discovery itself, it's the conditions that make discovery likely: the room, the question, the pause, the permission to not know yet.

I spent a decade doing that in physical space, for millions of people. Escape rooms. World Expo pavilions. A dinner staged in the toilets of a moving train. Then I got curious about curiosity itself, and spent a year inside 75+ designed conversations asking whether the same craft works at the scale of a life.

At the scale of a building

You design conditions for curiosity in space. Sightlines, thresholds, pacing, the moment of almost knowing.

At the scale of a life

Six practices design conditions for curiosity in attention. Observing, suspending, apprenticing, ritualizing, gracing, flowing.

Same discipline. Different medium.
