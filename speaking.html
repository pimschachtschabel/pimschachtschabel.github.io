Speaking & Workshops

Everyone is curious. Not every room lets them be.

Pim Schachtschabel has spent twelve years designing the world's most immersive experiences — from escape rooms that turn strangers into collaborators, to World Expo pavilions that move millions. He brings that same design thinking to the stage: every keynote is an experience, every workshop is a space carefully constructed for genuine discovery.

Drawing on his upcoming book Architecting Curiosity, his work at Tellart, and research with 75+ people across cultures, Pim offers audiences something rare — not just ideas to think about, but practices to embody.

Signature Talks

01

Designing Wonder

The 6 Principles That Make People Come Alive

We live in a world that optimizes for efficiency, certainty, and answers. But the most alive people, teams, and organizations share something that can't be optimized: wonder. Drawing on a decade of designing immersive experiences and research with 75+ people across cultures, Pim shows that nobody in the room is short on curiosity. What is missing is the conditions that let it show up, and those can be designed. This talk doesn't just change how audiences think about curiosity. It changes how they pay attention.

Ideal for: TEDx, creative conferences, design festivals, all-hands meetings

02

Beyond Certainty

Leading Through Uncertainty with the Architect's Mindset

The greatest challenge facing leaders today isn't a lack of strategy — it's a lack of curiosity. When uncertainty rises, our instinct is to reach for answers faster. But the organizations that navigate disruption most successfully are the ones that learn to stay in the question longer. Pim offers leaders the 6 Principles of Curiosity as a leadership operating system — embodied tools for navigating change by designing with it, not controlling it.

Ideal for: Leadership offsites, innovation summits, corporate keynotes, executive retreats

03

Re-Enchanting the Mundane

How to Make Magic in Everyday Experiences

What makes a train toilet a place of wonder? What turns a locked room into a journey of self-discovery? The answer isn't budget or technology — it's the design of curiosity. Pim opens his design toolkit and shares the principles behind experiences that transform the mundane. The audience doesn't just hear about it — they experience it, through a live exercise that proves wonder is never more than one design decision away.

Ideal for: Creative Mornings, design meetups, hospitality & events, educators
